# Precision Lattice

Precision Lattice treats an established structure as something to tune, not reinvent. Form remains faithful to its original hierarchy while spacing, line weight, and proportion are refined with painstaking care. The composition should feel familiar at first glance and unmistakably more resolved on inspection, as if a master designer spent countless hours correcting invisible imbalances.

Color works as a stable semantic lattice. Blue carries orchestration and return, green represents execution and verified knowledge, amber identifies human control boundaries, and gray contains safe refusal. Contrast is meticulously calibrated so each path can be followed without relying on decoration or excessive labels.

Typography establishes reading distance. Titles are confident, subtitles remain compact but comfortably legible, and technical English is treated as a secondary annotation rather than visual noise. Every baseline and line break must show painstaking attention, with master-level execution visible in the consistency of repeated components.

Connectors are part of the information, not an afterthought. Each arrow must begin and end decisively, branches must truly reconverge, and no line may merely suggest a relationship it fails to complete. The finished geometry should appear labored over with care, precise enough for both presentation and technical review.

The artifact should preserve information density while removing friction. Nothing new is added unless it clarifies an existing relationship; refinement comes from stronger hierarchy, cleaner rhythm, and exact alignment. The result must feel meticulously crafted, authoritative, and faithful to the source.
